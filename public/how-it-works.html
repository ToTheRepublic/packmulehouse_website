How X-Frame Works

Meet X-Frame from Pack Mule House. Hang it once and swap what you love.

Watch the short how-to, or read the steps below. You can mount the wall piece with screws or, if you'd rather not drill, with Command strips.

  1. Step 1

    Mark your holes

    Hold the leveling tool on the wall and press to mark your mounting holes.

  2. Step 2

    Mount it

    Fasten the wall mount at your marks.

    Or, for a no-drill option: put medium Command strips on the back of the wall mount, peel the liners, and press it onto your marks. It's mostly damage-free, but it won't adjust once it's stuck.

  3. Step 3

    Drop in the X-Frame

    Take the backing out of the frame and set the X-Frame in its place.

  4. Step 4

    Clip it in, or use the tabs

    Put a clip on each corner to hold the X-Frame in place. If your frame has tabs, press them down over the X-Frame instead.

  5. Step 5

    Hang it

    Hang it on the wall mount. That's it.

No frame needed

Peel and stick

Peel the liners and press the plate onto just about anything you want to hang. Hang it on the wall mount, just like a frame.

Then swap anytime

About five seconds

Whatever you stick it to fits the same mount. The photo swaps in about five seconds, with magnets and no tools.
