One-Click PDF: Privacy Policy

Published by Pack Mule House (packmulehouse.com) · Effective November 11, 2026

In short: One-Click PDF does not collect, store, or transmit your browsing data or the pages you save. PDFs are made on your own computer. If you choose to buy Pro, payment is handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe.

What the extension does

One-Click PDF ("the extension") saves the web page you're viewing as a PDF file in your Downloads folder, either as one continuous page or as standard pages. Optional Pro features hide ads, pop-ups and page clutter before the PDF is made.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not collect, record, or transmit the web pages you visit or save, their contents, your browsing history, or your search activity.
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, advertising identifiers, or cookies in the extension.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any personal data.

When you save a page, the extension uses your browser's built-in PDF engine on your own device. The page is briefly cleaned up (if you enabled that), turned into a PDF, and then restored. The PDF is written straight to your computer and is never uploaded.

Information stored on your device

The extension stores your settings (for example paper size and clean-up preferences) using your browser's extension storage. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned on, your browser may sync these settings between your devices. We never receive them.

Payments (Pro)

Pro is sold through ExtensionPay, which processes payments with Stripe.

  • To check whether Pro is active, the extension's ExtensionPay library contacts extensionpay.com using an anonymous, randomly generated key for your installation. No browsing data is included.
  • If you buy Pro, ExtensionPay collects your email address and Stripe collects your payment details on their own checkout pages. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your use of those services is covered by the ExtensionPay and Stripe privacy policies. As the seller, Pack Mule House may see the email address and purchase record of paying customers in its ExtensionPay/Stripe account, and uses them only to provide and support your purchase, process refunds, and meet legal and tax obligations.

Permissions

  • debugger: lets the extension use the browser's own PDF engine on the tab you click, so no print dialog is needed. It's attached only while a save runs.
  • downloads: saves the PDF to your Downloads folder.
  • contextMenus: adds the right-click menu.
  • storage: remembers your settings and Pro status.

Children

The extension is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be noted in the extension's store listing.
